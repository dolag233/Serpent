fix(fbx): flip V texture coordinates in the ufbx conversion (Serpent-a5ic)

The final cause of the "fragmented" texture rendering: FBX UVs have a
bottom-left origin while glTF has top-left (GLTFLoader sets flipY=false),
so without a V flip textures render upside-down and normal maps sample
wrong. Every mainstream FBX->glTF converter flips V by default
(Facebook FBX2glTF, ezhangle/FBX2glTF, cocos/FBX-glTF-conv) — the bridge
now writes v' = 1-v into the UV blob.

Excluded along the way: triangle geometry (ufbx_triangulate_face, kept —
fan was wrong for concave faces), UV set (100% identical to FBXLoader),
texture bytes (SHA-identical), normal maps (removal changed nothing).

WASM rebuilt, lock hash updated (glue unchanged); fbx-conversion 20/20;
user-verified on the reported model.
